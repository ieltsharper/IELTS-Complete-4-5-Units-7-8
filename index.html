3. Join the pairs of sentences using who, which, that or where. Sometimes two answers are possible.

  1. This is a photograph I took. It's of the desert.
    This is a photograph that/which I took of the desert.
  2. Riyadh is a city in Saudi Arabia. I grew up there.
    Riyadh is the ...
    Suggestion: Riyadh is the city in Saudi Arabia where I grew up.
  3. We rode horses in the country park. They were very beautiful.
    The horses ...
    Suggestion: The horses that/which we rode in the country park were very beautiful.
  4. People travel by train. They can enjoy the passing scenery.
    People ...
    Suggestion: People who/that travel by train can enjoy the passing scenery.
  5. Greece is a country. The first Olympic Games took place there.
    Greece is the ...
    Suggestion: Greece is the country where the first Olympic Games took place.
  6. Some tourists try new activities. More adventurous tourists do this.
    Tourists ...
    Suggestion: Tourists who/that try new activities are more adventurous. (Also: More adventurous tourists are the ones who try new activities.)
